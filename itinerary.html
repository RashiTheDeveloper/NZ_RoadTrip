11 Dec

Stay in Auckland.

Auckland, Coromandel - 1st night

Why: iconic coastal scenery and a geothermal beach you won't find anywhere else.

  • Auckland city highlights 90 min No ticket for a waterfront walk. Sky Tower is optional and needs a ticket if you go up.
  • Drive Auckland to Cathedral Cove about 2 hrs No ticket. Leave early. Summer parking often means a shuttle from Hahei (pay on the day).
  • Cathedral Cove walk 90 to 120 min No ticket. Go near low to mid tide. Track can close after storms: check DOC.
  • Hot Water Beach 45 to 60 min No ticket. Dig around low tide (about 2 hrs either side). Bring a spade, or hire one locally.

15 Dec

Stay in Wellington.

Taupo to Wellington (5th night)

Why:Discover Taupo's natural wonders before enjoying a scenic drive to Wellington, combining waterfalls, lakes, geothermal attractions, and spectacular countryside views.

  • Huka Falls 30 min No ticket. Short walk from the car park.
  • Lake Taupō lookout 30 to 60 min No ticket.
  • Craters of the Moon (optional) 60 min Pay at the gate. No need to prebook.
  • Drive Taupō to Wellington about 5 hrs No ticket. Leave by late morning.
16 Dec

Stay in Wellington.

Wellington (6th night)

Why: Explore New Zealand's capital through iconic attractions, panoramic viewpoints, and cultural treasures.

  • Te Papa Museum 2 to 3 hrs Museum entry is free. Some visiting exhibitions charge. No booking for general entry.
  • Parliament tour 60 min Book on the Parliament website. Bring photo ID. Skip if your ferry is a morning sailing.
  • Wellington Cable Car and Botanic Garden 45 to 75 min Buy a Cable Car ticket at the station. No need to prebook.
  • Mt Victoria lookout 45 min No ticket. Evening light over the harbour..
17 Dec

Stay in Nelson.

Wellington morning, ferry to Picton, then Nelson (7th night)

Travel from the North Island to the South Island on a scenic adventure featuring unforgettable ocean views, picturesque coastal towns, and incredible wildlife encounters.

  • Interislander ferry to Picton about 3.5 hrs Booked with the car. 8.15 AM.
  • Drive Picton to Nelson about 2 hrs Book a Nelson hotel for tonight.
  • Nelson centre, riverside, or Tahunanui Beach 90 min No ticket. Cafés and the cathedral steps are the easy version of Nelson.
18 Dec

Stay in Christchurch.

Nelson, then the Kaikōura coast (8th night)

Why: Sunshine city, then whales and seals country.

  • Drive Nelson to Kaikōura about 3.5 to 4 hrs No ticket. Stop at Ōhau Point seals on the way (20 min, no ticket, stay back from the rocks).
  • Tramway / Christchurch city centre Historic tram around central Christchurch to see the city without too much walking.
  • Willowbank Wildlife Reserve You can see New Zealand wildlife, including kiwi.
19 Dec

Stay in Lake Tekapo. OR Mt Cook OR Wanaka

Tekapo/Mt Cook/ Whanka (9th night)

Deep water wildlife, then glacial blue and dark skies.

  • Whale watching (boat or flight) 3 hrs Book now. Morning sailing is best. Weather cancels often: the operator usually rebooks or refunds. Flights are a backup ticket.
  • Seal colony lookout 30 min No ticket. Keep 20 metres away.
  • Drive Kaikōura to Lake Tekapo about 5 to 5.5 hrs
  • Tekapo Springs (optional) 2 hrs Buy tickets online or at the door. Hot pools do not need weeks of notice.
  • Lake Pukaki viewpoints 30 min No ticket. Several signed lookouts off SH8.
  • Drive to Wānaka about 2.5 hrs from Aoraki village No ticket via Lindis Pass.
  • That Wānaka Tree and lakefront 40 min No ticket. Do not climb the tree.
  • Stargazing tour (optonal) 2 hrs Book now. Dark Sky Project and similar tours fill in December. Cloud can cancel.

21 Dec

Queenstown day 2 of 4 (11th night)

Town, lake, and views without a long transfer.

  • Queenstown waterfront and town walk 60 min No ticket.
  • Skyline Gondola and optional luge 2 hrs Book now for a timed gondola in peak. Luge is extra tickets at the top.
  • TSS Earnslaw steamship cruise 1.5 to 2 hrs Book now. Farm barbecue sailings need a ticket. A short harbour cruise is the simpler option.
  • Optional helicopter flight 45 to 90 min Book now and keep it flexible: alpine weather cancels. Not essential if budget is tight.
22 Dec

Milford Sound- Queenstown day 3 of 4 · (12th night)

A full day out from Queenstown into Fiordland.

  • Scenic drive toward Milford (or a coach / fly option) about 4 hrs each way by road Book a day tour now if you do not want to self drive. Self drive: no road ticket, but leave by 6am. Check NZTA for Homer Tunnel delays.
  • Mirror Lakes 15 to 20 min No ticket. Short boardwalk.
  • The Chasm walk 20 to 30 min No ticket. Stay behind barriers.
  • Milford Sound / Piopiotahi cruise 2 to 2.5 hrs Book now. This is the must buy ticket of the South Island. Nature cruise or coach plus cruise packages both work.
23 Dec

Lord of the Rings Day-Glenorchy-Queenstown day 4 of 4

Lord of the Rings landscapes at the head of the lake.

  • Drive Queenstown to Glenorchy about 50 min No ticket. The lakeside road is the attraction. Go slowly.
  • Paradise Valley and Dart River views 60 min No ticket for lookouts. Some farm roads are private.
  • Lord of the Rings guided tour 4 to 5 hrs Book now. Guides get you onto locations you cannot reach alone.
  • Optional Dart River jet boat about 2.5 hrs including transfers Book now if you want it. Easy to skip if the LOTR tour is enough.
