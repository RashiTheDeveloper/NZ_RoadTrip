11 to 23 December

Day by day

Each activity is listed with estimated time on site. Drive times are extra. December is peak season, so anything marked Book now can sell out.

Base

Hamilton, 4 nights

Check in 11 Dec. Check out 15 Dec. Use it for Waitomo, Hobbiton, and Hamilton Gardens without packing the car every morning.

South Island

Nelson visit

Night of 17 Dec and a full morning on 18 Dec after the Interislander, before the Kaikōura coast.

Finish

Queenstown, 3 days

21, 22 and 23 Dec based in town: town and lake, Milford Sound, then Glenorchy.

Book before you fly

  • Interislander ferry (Wellington to Picton), 17 Dec
  • Waitomo Glowworm Caves, 13 Dec
  • Hobbiton Movie Set, 14 Dec
  • Māori cultural evening with hāngi, 15 Dec
  • Whale watch, Kaikōura, 19 Dec
  • Milford Sound cruise, 22 Dec
  • Lord of the Rings / Glenorchy tour, 23 Dec
11 Dec

Auckland, Coromandel, then Hamilton night 1

Coastal first day, then settle in Hamilton for four nights.

  • Auckland city highlights 90 min No ticket for a waterfront walk. Sky Tower is optional and needs a ticket if you go up.
  • Drive Auckland to Cathedral Cove about 2 hrs No ticket. Leave early. Summer parking often means a shuttle from Hahei (pay on the day).
  • Cathedral Cove walk 90 to 120 min No ticket. Go near low to mid tide. Track can close after storms: check DOC.
  • Hot Water Beach 45 to 60 min No ticket. Dig around low tide (about 2 hrs either side). Bring a spade, or hire one locally.
  • Driving Creek Railway 90 min Book now. Timed train seats in December.
  • Drive to Hamilton, check in about 2.5 hrs Night 1 of 4 in Hamilton.
12 Dec

Hamilton at an easier pace

A recovery day in the city you are using as a base.

  • Hamilton Gardens 90 min to 2 hrs Grounds are free. Some enclosed gardens may ask a donation. No booking needed.
  • Waikato Museum or riverside walk 60 to 90 min Museum has a door charge. Book only if you want a guided tour.
  • Free afternoon in Hamilton open Laundry, groceries, swim at Hamilton Lake. Night 2 of 4.
13 Dec

Waitomo day trip from Hamilton

Glowworms without changing hotel.

  • Drive Hamilton to Waitomo about 1 hr No ticket.
  • Waitomo Glowworm Caves (boat through the grotto) 45 to 60 min Book now. Choose a timed entry. Black water rafting is a separate ticket if you want the adventure version (about 3 to 5 hrs).
  • Lunch in Waitomo village 45 min No booking required for cafés, but weekends queue.
  • Return to Hamilton about 1 hr Night 3 of 4.
14 Dec

Hobbiton day trip from Hamilton

The Shire is an easy loop from Kirikiriroa.

  • Drive Hamilton to Hobbiton (Matamata) about 50 min No ticket for the drive.
  • Hobbiton Movie Set tour 2.5 to 3 hrs Book now. You cannot walk in without a tour ticket. Banquet upgrade is extra and also books out.
  • Optional Matamata village stop 30 min No ticket.
  • Return to Hamilton about 50 min Night 4 of 4. Last Hamilton night.
15 Dec

Check out Hamilton, Rotorua culture, then Taupō

Geothermal colour and a hāngi on the way south.

  • Drive Hamilton to Wai-O-Tapu about 1 hr 20 min No ticket for the drive.
  • Wai-O-Tapu Thermal Wonderland 2.5 to 3 hrs Book now in December or arrive at opening. Lady Knox geyser is about 10:15am: be inside the park before that.
  • Te Puia (geysers, carving, kiwi house) 2 to 3 hrs Book now. Combo tickets with a cultural show exist. Pōhutu geyser is the headline.
  • Māori cultural village and hāngi dinner about 4 hrs Book now. Tamaki or Mitai style evenings sell out in December. Starts late afternoon.
  • Drive to Taupō, overnight about 1 hr Book a Taupō hotel. A same day push to Wellington after hāngi is too late and too far.
16 Dec

Taupō to Wellington

The caldera lake, then a full afternoon in the capital.

  • Huka Falls 30 min No ticket. Short walk from the car park.
  • Lake Taupō lookout 30 to 60 min No ticket.
  • Craters of the Moon (optional) 60 min Pay at the gate. No need to prebook.
  • Drive Taupō to Wellington about 5 hrs No ticket. Leave by late morning.
  • Mt Victoria lookout 45 min No ticket. Evening light over the harbour.
17 Dec

Wellington morning, ferry, then Nelson

Capital highlights, then the South Island via Picton.

  • Te Papa Tongarewa 2 to 3 hrs Museum entry is free. Some visiting exhibitions charge. No booking for general entry.
  • Parliament tour 60 min Book now on the Parliament website. Bring photo ID. Skip if your ferry is a morning sailing.
  • Wellington Cable Car and Botanic Garden 45 to 75 min Buy a Cable Car ticket at the station. No need to prebook.
  • Interislander ferry to Picton about 3.5 hrs Book now, with the car. December sailings fill. Check in at least 60 min before departure.
  • Drive Picton to Nelson about 2 hrs No ticket. Book a Nelson hotel for tonight.
18 Dec

Nelson, then the Kaikōura coast

Sunshine city, then whales and seals country.

  • Nelson centre, riverside, or Tahunanui Beach 90 min No ticket. Cafés and the cathedral steps are the easy version of Nelson.
  • Optional Abel Tasman water taxi or short walk 3 to 5 hrs Book now if you want a water taxi or kayak from Kaiteriteri / Mārahau. Skip this if you need an earlier run to Kaikōura.
  • Drive Nelson to Kaikōura about 3.5 to 4 hrs No ticket. Stop at Ōhau Point seals on the way (20 min, no ticket, stay back from the rocks).
  • Kaikōura Peninsula Walkway 60 to 90 min No ticket. Free coastal track. Overnight Kaikōura.
19 Dec

Kaikōura whales, then Lake Tekapo

Deep water wildlife, then glacial blue and dark skies.

  • Whale watching (boat or flight) 3 hrs Book now. Morning sailing is best. Weather cancels often: the operator usually rebooks or refunds. Flights are a backup ticket.
  • Seal colony lookout 30 min No ticket. Keep 20 metres away.
  • Drive Kaikōura to Lake Tekapo about 5 to 5.5 hrs No ticket. Fuel in Kaikōura or Culverden. This is a long afternoon.
  • Church of the Good Shepherd 20 min No ticket. Be quiet if a service or wedding is on. Do not walk in the flower beds.
  • Tekapo Springs (optional) 2 hrs Buy tickets online or at the door. Hot pools do not need weeks of notice.
  • Stargazing tour 2 hrs Book now. Dark Sky Project and similar tours fill in December. Cloud can cancel.
20 Dec

Aoraki / Mt Cook and Wānaka, arrive Queenstown

Alpine day, then check in for the Queenstown stay.

  • Lake Pukaki viewpoints 30 min No ticket. Several signed lookouts off SH8.
  • Hooker Valley Track 3 hrs return No ticket. DOC track. Start early. Pay and display or use the app for White Horse Hill parking. Closed in heavy weather.
  • Drive to Wānaka about 2.5 hrs from Aoraki village No ticket via Lindis Pass.
  • That Wānaka Tree and lakefront 40 min No ticket. Do not climb the tree.
  • Drive Wānaka to Queenstown, check in about 1 hr 15 min Book Queenstown lodging for nights 20, 21 and 22 Dec (three nights, three full days after this).
21 Dec

Queenstown day 1 of 3

Town, lake, and views without a long transfer.

  • Queenstown waterfront and town walk 60 min No ticket.
  • Skyline Gondola and optional luge 2 hrs Book now for a timed gondola in peak. Luge is extra tickets at the top.
  • TSS Earnslaw steamship cruise 1.5 to 2 hrs Book now. Farm barbecue sailings need a ticket. A short harbour cruise is the simpler option.
  • Optional helicopter flight 45 to 90 min Book now and keep it flexible: alpine weather cancels. Not essential if budget is tight.
22 Dec

Queenstown day 2 of 3 · Milford Sound

A full day out from Queenstown into Fiordland.

  • Scenic drive toward Milford (or a coach / fly option) about 4 hrs each way by road Book a day tour now if you do not want to self drive. Self drive: no road ticket, but leave by 6am. Check NZTA for Homer Tunnel delays.
  • Mirror Lakes 15 to 20 min No ticket. Short boardwalk.
  • The Chasm walk 20 to 30 min No ticket. Stay behind barriers.
  • Milford Sound / Piopiotahi cruise 2 to 2.5 hrs Book now. This is the must buy ticket of the South Island. Nature cruise or coach plus cruise packages both work.
23 Dec

Queenstown day 3 of 3 · Glenorchy

Lord of the Rings landscapes at the head of the lake.

  • Drive Queenstown to Glenorchy about 50 min No ticket. The lakeside road is the attraction. Go slowly.
  • Paradise Valley and Dart River views 60 min No ticket for lookouts. Some farm roads are private.
  • Lord of the Rings guided tour 4 to 5 hrs Book now. Guides get you onto locations you cannot reach alone.
  • Optional Dart River jet boat about 2.5 hrs including transfers Book now if you want it. Easy to skip if the LOTR tour is enough.

Top experiences that need a ticket

  1. Milford Sound cruise
  2. Hobbiton Movie Set
  3. Waitomo Glowworm Caves
  4. Māori cultural evening with hāngi
  5. Wai-O-Tapu (timed for Lady Knox)
  6. Whale watching, Kaikōura
  7. Interislander with the car
  8. Lake Tekapo stargazing
  9. Queenstown gondola / Earnslaw / helicopter
  10. Glenorchy LOTR tour

Times are time at the place, not the whole day. Always recheck ferry and alpine roads the night before.
